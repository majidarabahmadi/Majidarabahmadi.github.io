ABOUT

I started in high-voltage design — building the control and protection schematics that tell a substation how to behave under fault. Since 2021 I've worked inside Sarcheshmeh Copper, one of Iran's largest mining complexes, where electrical design has to keep pace with a live industrial site: coordinating engineering, QC, and subcontractors so drawings survive contact with fabrication and installation.

Research runs alongside the fieldwork. I've published on microgrid protection and the grid-code questions raised by inverter-based resources — the kind of problems that HV substations will need answers to as renewables reshape the grid.
